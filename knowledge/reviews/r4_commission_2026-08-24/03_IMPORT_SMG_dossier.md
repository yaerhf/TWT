## 0. Why this dossier exists

TWT's ontology is a **local bosonic substrate** (orientation/rotor field on a 4D lattice) from which chiral fermionic matter is supposed to emerge as defects. Independently of every other claim in the corpus, that ontological commitment walks into a forty-year literature with two faces: a graveyard of failed attempts to put chiral fermions on a lattice, and a modern conjecture that says exactly when such attempts can succeed. Both faces bear on TWT with unusual directness, because the modern success condition is an anomaly ledger, and TWT's matter sector either satisfies it or does not — a checkable structural fact, not a matter of taste.

## 1. The obstruction half

**I-SMG-1 (Nielsen–Ninomiya). [KNOWN]** A local, translation-invariant, Hermitian **free** lattice fermion theory in 3+1D cannot produce a net-chiral spectrum: doublers appear with opposite chirality (Nielsen & Ninomiya, Nucl. Phys. B 185 (1981) 20; B 193 (1981) 173). *Level applied:* substrate. *Consequence for TWT:* any free-field reading of "defect = chiral fermion" is forbidden; whatever produces TWT's chiral spectrum must be interacting from the start. This theorem is why the rest of this dossier exists.

**I-SMG-2 (Eichten–Preskill, and its failure in the original form).** Eichten & Preskill, Nucl. Phys. B 268 (1986) 179 [SNIP], proposed gapping the unwanted mirror/doubler modes with strong multi-fermion interactions, keeping the chiral half light — the ancestral SMG idea; the modern review says plainly that the idea of gapping massless fermions by interaction dates back to this paper as an attempt to regularize lattice chiral fermions (Wang & You, *Symmetry* 14 (2022) 1475, arXiv:2204.14271 [ABS+SNIP]). The original program **failed in its own model**: Golterman, Petcher & Rivas, "Absence of chiral fermions in the Eichten–Preskill model," Nucl. Phys. B 395 (1993) 596 [SNIP — title-level result confirmed across multiple citing sources], showed the phase structure does not deliver the advertised chiral continuum limit. Later mirror-decoupling attempts with Ginsparg–Wilson fermions also returned negatives in the cases tested: Chen, Giedt & Poppitz, "On the decoupling of mirror fermions," JHEP 04 (2013) 131 [SNIP]; Kikukawa, PTEP 2019 073B02 (arXiv:1710.11101) [SNIP], arguing the mirror sector of the tested 2d abelian models does not decouple.

**I-SMG-3 (the live dispute, present tense).** The paradigm's application to chiral **gauge** theories is contested right now: Golterman & Shamir, "Propagator zeros and lattice chiral gauge theories," PRL 132 (2024) 081903 [SNIP], and a 2026 PRD follow-up ("Constraints on the symmetric mass generation paradigm for lattice chiral gauge theories") [SNIP] press on whether the SMG phase's composite states do what the proposal needs; Kikukawa's PTEP 2019 113B03 (arXiv:1710.11618) [SNIP] raises the gauge-invariant measure problem for overlap Weyl fermions in the 16 of SO(10). *Import discipline:* TWT must cite the conjecture below **as a conjecture with named opposition**, not as a theorem.

## 2. The precedent half

**I-SMG-4 (the anomaly-free condition — the conjecture itself).** The modern statement: chiral fermion content can be non-perturbatively regularized on a lattice in the same dimension — equivalently, the mirror sector can be symmetrically gapped — **iff the content is free of all 't Hooft anomalies, perturbative local and non-perturbative global**. Wen's summary of his own construction states that any truly anomaly-free chiral gauge theory can be non-perturbatively defined by putting it on a lattice in the same dimension (Wen, Chin. Phys. Lett. 30 (2013) 111101, arXiv:1305.1045 [ABS+SNIP]); Wang & Wen, "A non-perturbative definition of the standard models," PRR 2 (2020) 023356, arXiv:1809.11171 [ABS+SNIP], propose that Spin(10) chiral fermion theories with Weyl fermions in the 16-dimensional spinor representation can be defined on a 3+1D lattice without doubling — via the cobordism classification of anomalies and symmetric gapped boundaries for the mirror sector — while the 15n (no-ν_R) Standard Models require extra structure. A 2025 lattice status report states the necessary condition as cancellation of all continuous and discrete 't Hooft anomalies, occurring in 3+1D for multiples of 16 Weyl fermions (arXiv:2511.22678 [ABS]).

**I-SMG-5 (the 16 and where it comes from).** Condensed-matter root: interaction-induced collapse of free-fermion classifications — Fidkowski & Kitaev's ℤ₈ in 1D (PRB 81 (2010) 134509) [KNOWN], the 3D "16-fold way" ℤ → ℤ₁₆ collapse for class-DIII topological superconductors (Metlitski, Fidkowski, Chen & Vishwanath, arXiv:1406.3032) [SNIP]. Particle-physics form: You, BenTov & Xu, arXiv:1402.4151 [ABS], open with the observation that the Standard Model **plus a right-handed neutrino** has precisely 16 Weyl fermions per generation, and construct 3+1D chiral theories regularizable on a 3D spatial lattice **when and only when** the flavor count is a multiple of 16. Cobordism form: García-Etxebarria & Montero, "Dai–Freed anomalies in particle physics," JHEP 08 (2019) 003, arXiv:1808.00009 [SNIP], compute the ℤ₁₆-valued global anomaly of Spin×_{ℤ₂}ℤ₄ backgrounds: with the discrete ℤ₄ ⊂ B−L-type structure held exact, each Weyl of unit charge contributes 1 mod 16, so a 15-Weyl generation carries a residual −1 mod 16 and a 16-Weyl generation cancels. Wen's own lattice SM uses 48 = 3×16 Weyl fermions (arXiv:1305.1045 [ABS]).

**I-SMG-6 (constructive evidence that the mechanism is real).** 1+1D: the anomaly-free chiral 3-4-5-0 model is numerically demonstrated to undergo SMG (Zeng, Zhu, Wang & You, PRL 128 (2022) 185301, arXiv:2202.12355) [SNIP]; the anomaly-matching ⇔ gapping-rules equivalence is Wang & Wen, arXiv:1307.7480 / PRB 107 (2023) 014311 [SNIP]. 3+1D: lattice SMG phases exist and are under active study — Ayyar & Chandrasekharan (PRD 91 (2015) 065035; PRD 93 (2016) 081701) [SNIP]; Butt, Catterall & Schaich, PRD 98 (2018) 114514; Catterall's staggered/Kähler–Dirac line (arXiv:2010.02290; PRD 104 (2021) 014503) [SNIP]; Butt, Catterall & Hasenfratz, PRL 134 (2025) 031602 [SNIP]; SU(3) with N_f = 8 showing continuum-limit SMG evidence (arXiv:2511.22678 [ABS]). Field-theory side: Razamat & Tong, "Gapped chiral fermions," PRX 11 (2021) 011063 [SNIP], exhibit symmetry-preserving gapped phases for anomaly-free chiral matter in 4d.

## 3. What TWT's matter sector must satisfy — the precise statement

Let the per-generation wave-coupled chiral content of a TWT candidate be the 15-Weyl spectrum of §C.2.8 plus the sterile S₋ partner of §C.3.12 (16 Weyl), with hypercharges as derived (R-056/057/062, P4–P7) and weak isospin on the §C.4.2 host. For the SMG precedent to run **in TWT's favor** — i.e., for "chiral matter from a local bosonic substrate" to be the kind of thing the modern literature licenses rather than the kind the older literature buries — **all** of the following must hold, generation by generation:

**(A) Perturbative local anomalies of the gauged factors vanish.** With colour ℤ₃-discrete and ungauged in TWT, the continuous gauged factors are SU(2)₊ and U(1)_Y. Required: [SU(2)]²U(1)_Y, [U(1)_Y]³, and grav²·U(1)_Y all cancel. These are the standard hypercharge sum rules; they hold for the SM table, and TWT's charge arithmetic reproduces the SM table, so they hold — but they are currently **implicit**. Proposed engine primitive: compute the three sums over the 16-Weyl spectrum and assert zero, with the counterfactual (any single P7-style slot flip breaks at least one sum). Cheap, bankable, and it converts "we reproduce SM charges" into "we satisfy the anomaly ledger" as a checked fact.

**(B) The Witten SU(2) global anomaly vanishes.** The number of SU(2)₊ doublets per generation must be even; TWT has 3 (colour states) + 1 = 4. Also trivially bankable as a primitive (doublet count mod 2), and worth banking because it is a place where a variant candidate could silently die.

**(C) The ℤ₁₆ Spin×_{ℤ₂}ℤ₄ global anomaly vanishes — and this is where TWT's Dirac-neutrino structure carries real weight.** TWT claims exact B−L conservation with structurally forbidden Majorana masses (R-089, §C.5.6), so the discrete ℤ₄ remnant relevant to the García-Etxebarria–Montero computation is **exact** in TWT — the constraint applies with full force, and a 15-Weyl generation would carry an uncancelled −1 mod 16. TWT passes **iff the sterile S₋ partner is a genuine dynamical Weyl fermion per generation** — which §C.3.12/R-121 asserts (three sterile right-handed neutrinos). Consistency statement worth recording at full strength: **the same structural fact (exact B−L ⇒ Dirac neutrinos ⇒ ν_R exists) that TWT derived for its own internal reasons is precisely the condition the bosonic-UV-completion literature independently demands.** The corpus's 16-with-sterile count (§C.3.13) equals You–BenTov–Xu's 16 and Wen's 48/3 exactly. This is the strongest external consonance the matter sector has, and it is falsifiable in-corpus: any future variant that demotes S₋ from a dynamical Weyl to a bookkeeping label loses the bosonic-substrate license.

**(D) Discrete-colour anomalies — an open computation TWT owes and can actually do.** Because TWT's colour is a discrete ℤ₃ rather than gauged SU(3), the usual [SU(3)]-family anomaly conditions are moot, but **discrete symmetries carry their own 't Hooft/Dai–Freed anomalies**, and the cobordism toolkit of I-SMG-5 applies to them directly. Required: the ℤ₃ colour action on the 16-Weyl spectrum (three trivector seats cyclically permuted, lepton seat fixed) must be anomaly-free in the appropriate cobordism group, alone and mixed with Spin structure and U(1)_Y. I did not find this computed anywhere in the corpus. It is finite, well-posed, and standard-toolkit; **proposed as a new open item with a retirement handle** (compute the relevant bordism group for Spin × ℤ₃ × U(1) with TWT's assignment; the Dai–Freed η-invariant machinery of arXiv:1808.00009 is the template). Outcome matters in both directions: pass and the substrate license is complete; fail and the ℤ₃-discrete-colour pick — one of the family's most distinctive commitments — has an anomaly obstruction nobody has noticed.

**(E) The conjecture itself is imported at conjecture strength.** Even with (A)–(D) all green, what the literature then licenses is: *no known obstruction* to a local bosonic regularization of TWT's matter content, with constructive existence demonstrated in 1+1D and evidenced-but-contested in 3+1D (I-SMG-3). Green lights here make TWT's ontology **possible**, not achieved; the corpus's own gauge-boson and dynamics gates remain untouched by this dossier.

## 5. What this dossier does not license

It does not supply gauge bosons, dynamics, or the kernel; it does not upgrade any FIT or CANDIDATE row; it does not make the substrate's fermion-emergence mechanism concrete — it only locates the exact ledger that mechanism must eventually satisfy, shows that TWT's declared spectrum sits on the favorable side of the sharpest known entry (the mod-16 line), and names the one entry (discrete colour) still uncomputed.
