## 1. The convergent verdict: DOES-NOT-APPLY-AS-PROVED, MECHANISM SURVIVES AS A DESIGN CONSTRAINT

The human's forecast was right on both counts — there IS an initial category boundary, and the
theorem might not apply — with the referees locating it precisely and independently:

- **The failing hypothesis, named by both.** GS assumption (1): *"The hamiltonian has a finite
  range, and depends on fermion fields only"* (§III). On a bosonic substrate this is not false
  but **undefined** — it predicates a property of objects the theory does not contain. The
  theorem's central object, `H_eff(p) = ℛ⁻¹(p)`, inverts a **fermion two-point function** built
  from retarded anticommutators; on this substrate there is nothing to invert. (B's finding;
  A confirms hypothesis (1) is load-bearing through CAR ⇒ bounded operator norm ⇒ the whole
  analyticity apparatus.)
- **The reach question, settled against over-reading in either direction.** Neither primary
  claims reach over non-fermionic microscopic degrees of freedom: the reach language is
  expectation-grade ("one expects", "experience teaches us") and the one *named* extension is
  to Euclidean **fermionic** path-integral formulations (A). GS themselves scope the theorem —
  *"all of its assumptions need to be satisfied … case by case"* — and, decisively, **exhibit a
  local model containing a bosonic field where their own theorem fails and the spectrum is
  chiral** (their gauge-fixing approach, §III): the authors' own demonstration that the
  no-boson hypothesis is load-bearing. Independent corroboration with no stake in TWT:
  Lu–Seifnashri–Shao (arXiv:2604.06307) state such no-goes "are evaded by using lattice bosons
  rather than fermions."
- **Where the theorem's strong-coupling power comes from (A):** not from propagator zeros (the
  obstruction) but from a Lieb–Robinson-type bound (finite range + finite operator norm) plus
  hypothesis (2) — *no massless bosons* — which forces residual interactions irrelevant. The
  power over strong coupling is **borrowed from (2), not earned** — and a rotor substrate
  carries massless bosons nearly by construction (B: GS concede they invalidate the required
  smoothness of `H_eff`). Fragile step: Eq. (24)'s smoothness-at-singularities is argued at
  one-loop leading-log, not proved. Fragile hypothesis: (3), the zero-free interpolating basis,
  conjecture-supported and dented by Baig–Chen–Cherman–Neuzil (arXiv:2607.09935 — a healthy
  ultra-local bosonic model whose reconstructed Dirac operator is doubler-free but robustly
  non-local).

## 2. What does NOT survive of our defences — the referees' findings against us

1. **"Our fermions are composite" is DEAD as a defence** — GS explicitly permit composite
   interpolating fields and adopt SMG bound states as their central mechanism (a reversal from
   the PRL's dismissal, conceded in their own footnote 12). The category failure lives at the
   *fundamental variables* and at `ℛ`'s constructibility, nowhere shallower.
2. **"Our colour is discrete" is DEAD as a defence** — the theorem needs only discrete-valued
   conserved charges; ℤ₃ is inside its bookkeeping.
3. **Wang–Wen-class bosonic-realization claims may NOT be cited as a defence** without their own
   registered import — as theorems the two literatures have disjoint domains (no contradiction);
   as physics claims the tension is genuine and unresolved; as a TWT defence it would be an
   unregistered load-bearing import (B).
4. **The surviving skeleton is serious.** Strip the Grassmann variables and a degree argument
   remains (periodic BZ + smooth hermitian one-particle `H_eff` + charge grading + linear nodes
   ⇒ vector-like) — the same class that already bit this programme once (the Fermi-point route,
   refuted on our own substrate by our own spectral computation). A's crack C1 cuts the same
   way: much of the proof needs only *bounded local operators of finite range*, not fermionic
   ones. The evasions must be **positively exhibited**, and are priced in B's file (E1 the
   non-local winding interpolating operator — most natural, currently unargued; E2 the massless
   bosons GS concede — cheap but owing a phenomenological account).
