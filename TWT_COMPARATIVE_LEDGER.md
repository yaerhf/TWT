## How to read this ledger

**The criterion is DEBT STRUCTURE, never posit count** (RUL-037; ACTION_PLAN design
constraint). Three questions are asked of every line, of every framework:

1. **Named?** — is the debt stated as a debt, or absorbed into the framework's silence?
2. **In-principle payable?** — is there a route by which it *could* be discharged?
3. **ROUTE-VIABLE?** — is there a **named and unblocked** discharge route? *(Normalized 2026-08-18
   from the founding battery's third question, "Being worked?", under external-review catch 3.)*

**Why question 3 changed, and what the change costs.** *"Being worked"* measures **person-hours**,
and person-hours track community size, which tracks **incumbency**. Bohm's *"worked since 1992"* is a
research literature; TWT's is one coordinator and a set of agents. A battery scoring effort rewards
the incumbent for being populous and penalizes a challenger for being small — **a sociological fact
recorded as if it were an epistemic one.** But the replacement must be priced like any other
yardstick (role file, YARDSTICK PRICING), and it is not neutral either: **route-viability is
self-assessed by whoever owns the route, so it flatters challengers exactly where "being worked"
flattered incumbents.** Three guards, all binding:

- a route counts as viable only if, where it is obstructed, **the blocker is named**;
- every cell carries one of the fixed grades below, so a reader can check the grade against the
  cited record rather than against a narrative;
- **the instruments are asymmetric and the ledger says so**: TWT's grades are auditable against its
  negatives ledger (*tried → failed → would-change-if*), while the rivals' are read from the
  literature at **SURVEYED** depth. That is an asymmetry of *instrument*, not of *effort*, and it
  cuts both ways — TWT's cells are more checkable **and** self-produced.

**Effort has not stopped mattering.** It is recorded where it belongs: as a remark inside the cell
(*"two live programmes, thirty-plus years"*; *"one probe session, one reviewed candidate"*), never as
the battery's question. **The remark is owed on every side of a line, not only on TWT's** — a battery
that carries *"one reviewed candidate"* on the challenger's cell and nothing on the incumbent's has
smuggled the old bias back in as silence.

**THE CLOSED GRADE VOCABULARY** *(enumerated and closed 2026-08-18 under contra-review revision 12:
the first application of this battery used **nine grades that were not on the original list**, which
is an open vocabulary, i.e. a narrative — the precise thing guard 2 exists to prevent. Every cell in
this document now carries one of the ten below and nothing else.)*

**Not-owed grades** — no route question arises:
`N/A-PAID` (discharged in ontology) · `N/A-DELIVERED` (the construction was built — effort remark
required) · `N/A-DENIED` (the framework denies the explanandum) · `N/A-NOT-ATTEMPTED` (no claim
requiring it).

**Owed grades**, worst last:
`VIABLE-UNBLOCKED` (named route, no known blocker) · `NAMED-BUT-UNSTARTED` (named, unblocked, and no
work begun anywhere) · `VIABLE-GATED` (blocked on a named internal object — for TWT, usually §D.5) ·
`NAMED-BUT-BLOCKED` (named, and its known instances computed closed — **the worst grade**;
the blocker is named in the cell) · `NONE-BY-DESIGN` (no route, and specifying one would change the
framework — this covers refusal) · `CONTESTED` (a route exists, is worked, and its adequacy is
disputed in the literature).

**Two orderings deliberately NOT asserted.** (1) **`CONTESTED` is not ranked against the VIABLE
grades.** A route that is undisputed *because nothing has been delivered* is **not** thereby better
than one disputed after thirty years of scrutiny; placing a challenger's bespoke grade above an
incumbent's `CONTESTED` would be the incumbent-penalty this normalization was introduced to remove,
running the other way. (2) **The not-owed grades are not "wins".** `N/A-NOT-ATTEMPTED` records
abstention, which finding 15 already books as neither discharge nor debt.

A posit count is deliberately not computed. Entity count favours TWT; ideological parsimony
(a lattice, a preferred foliation, a second time parameter) does not; and emergent-for-exact
Lorentz invariance is a cost under any accounting that prices symmetry. A count-based ledger
loses on inspection and hands a reviewer the counterexample.

**Symmetric or nothing.** No line is recorded on one side without the matching line at equal
depth on every framework in the comparison — including in TWT's favour. Where equal depth was
not achievable in this campaign, the line is demoted to **SURVEYED** and labelled, not shipped
as a half-itemized audit.

**The reference class is the interpretation league: Copenhagen, Bohm, Everett.** The Standard
Model enters as *target*, never as rival ontology (SCOPE_DECLARATION §1, applying RUL-037(i)).
No SM-facing debt comparison appears in Part I.

The line decomposes into **three** sub-debts that the one-phrase compression "everyone has a
measurement problem" destroys. Frameworks separate differently on each.

| Sub-debt | Copenhagen | Bohm | Everett | TWT |
