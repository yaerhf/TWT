**N54 — is the DYNAMICAL reading of the `Λ ~ H²` residual (`ρ_vac ∝ H(t)²` at ALL epochs) viable? → NO. EXCLUDED. (2026-07-29; review item II-10; arithmetic + primary-source bounds, no simulation.)**

- TRIED: taking §B.7.4/§E.1.1's `Λ ~ H²` at face value as a *dynamical law* — `ρ_vac(t) = 3ν M̄_Pl² H(t)²` holding at every epoch — propagating it through the flat Friedmann constraint, and confronting the result with CMB, BBN and expansion-history data.
- FAILED BECAUSE — the ansatz is self-defeating **before any data is consulted**. Substituting into `3 M̄_Pl² H² = ρ_m + ρ_r + ρ_vac` gives `3 M̄_Pl² H² (1−ν) = ρ_m + ρ_r`, hence **`Ω_vac(z) ≡ ν` identically**: the vacuum *fraction* is epoch-independent. Confrontations, in descending order of how much they rest on:
  1. **Early dark energy — the direct one.** Planck 2015 XIV (arXiv:1502.01590), *abstract*: early DE "has to be below ≈2% of the critical density even when forced to play a role for `z<50` only" — its *weakest* case. Flagged honestly — a `w = −1` component is not radiation, so this is an equivalent-energy translation quoted to size the violation; the verdict does not rest on it.
